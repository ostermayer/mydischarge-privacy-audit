Privacy Model

MyDischarge is designed so raw patient-identifying data is removed before language-model processing. The tests below verify the current app redactor against synthetic discharge paperwork with canary tokens and known PHI fields.
  • Photos stay on device. OCR reads discharge paperwork locally; image files are not uploaded for parsing.
  • Patient identifiers are redacted on device. The app removes names, dates of birth, ages, SSNs, patient phone numbers, patient email addresses, MRNs, insurance IDs, account numbers, and home addresses.
  • Clinical utility is preserved where possible. Diagnosis, medications, doctor names, hospital names, hospital phone numbers, and follow-up dates are intentionally preserved when they are not patient identifiers.
  • Network requests are de-identified. The egress tests simulate what would be sent after client redaction and the server-side safety barrier.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs are used in the app. Local session data is stored on-device in encrypted storage.
